Getting around

Public transport

Getting around Helsinki is easy with the HSL app (Helsingin seudun liikenne / Helsinki Region Transport). It covers buses, trams, the metro and the Suomenlinna ferry, with live timetables, journey planning and the ability to buy and store tickets right on your phone — no need to queue for paper tickets.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

The ferry to Suomenlinna leaves from the Market Square (Kauppatori) terminal in central Helsinki, and is run by HSL — the same app and ticket you'd use for buses and trams.

Please check the HSL app for departures from Kauppatori to get to Suomenlinna, and from Suomenlinna to return.

  • Remember to bring any identification required for your flights, hotels and ferry journey.
  • Whatever you choose to wear for the wedding, please plan for the cold journey to and from Suomenlinna with warm outerwear and practical shoes.
